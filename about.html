✦ Our Story

Built on Purpose,
Driven by Results

EcoTech Solutions was born in 2014 from a simple but powerful idea: that technology should be accessible, impactful, and beautifully crafted for every business.

Founded by a team of engineers, designers, and strategists in Silicon Valley, we started with three clients and a bold vision. Today, we're a 120-person team serving over 350 clients across 40+ countries. Our growth has been fueled by one thing: delivering results that genuinely transform businesses.

We've worked across industries — from healthcare and fintech to e-commerce and education — and every project has made us sharper, more creative, and more committed to excellence.

✦ Our Journey

A Decade of Milestones

From a small startup to a global technology leader — here's how we got here.

2014

Founded in Silicon Valley

EcoTech Solutions founded by 4 engineers with a vision to democratize digital excellence for all businesses.

2016

First Major Enterprise Client

Landed our first Fortune 500 contract, delivering a mission-critical cloud migration that saved the client $2M annually.

2018

Mobile Division Launch

Expanded our capabilities with a dedicated mobile development team. Launched 30+ apps in the first year alone.

2020

AI & Automation Practice

Launched our AI/ML division during the global pandemic, helping clients automate critical processes and adapt to remote-first work.

2022

Global Expansion

Opened offices in London, Singapore, and Dubai. Crossed $50M annual revenue milestone and reached 250+ clients in 30+ countries.

2024

10 Years of Excellence

Celebrated our 10th anniversary with 120+ team members, 350+ global clients, and recognition as a Top 50 Global Tech Agency by Forbes.
